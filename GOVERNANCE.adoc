= Project Governance

== Roles

* *Maintainers* have write access to the repository, review and merge pull requests, and make releases.
  Currently, the lead maintainer is https://github.com/lprimak[Lenny Primak] of Flow Logix, Inc.
* *Contributors* are anyone who submits issues, pull requests or documentation.
  See link:CONTRIBUTING.adoc[CONTRIBUTING.adoc].

== Decision making

Day-to-day decisions are made by the maintainers through issues and pull requests.
Significant changes, such as ending support for a version line, are discussed publicly in
https://github.com/flowlogix/flowlogix/discussions[GitHub Discussions] before being decided by the maintainers.

== Becoming a maintainer

Contributors with a sustained record of high-quality contributions may be invited by the existing maintainers.
Access is granted by the repository owner and is reviewed periodically.
